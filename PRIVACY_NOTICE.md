## 2. What Information Does PrintBit Collect?

Depending on the features you use, PrintBit may process the following information:

### A. Transaction Information

This may include:

- Selected printing, copying, or scanning service
- Number of pages, copies, or sheets
- Paper size and printing options
- Amount paid and payment-related transaction records
- Date and time of the transaction
- Transaction status, including completed, cancelled, failed, or interrupted transactions

### B. Files and Documents

When you use PrintBit's printing, copying, or scanning features, the system may temporarily process:

- Documents you upload for printing
- Images or other files submitted for printing
- Documents produced through scanning
- Copies of documents processed by the kiosk
- Information contained within those documents

Documents may contain personal information belonging to you or other individuals. You are responsible for ensuring that you have the appropriate authority to submit a document for processing.

### C. Technical and Operational Information

PrintBit may also process technical information necessary to operate and maintain the kiosk, such as:

- Device and system status
- Printing, scanning, and hardware errors
- Transaction-processing logs
- Application logs
- Service availability and operational events
- Basic diagnostic information

PrintBit does not intentionally collect unrelated personal information merely because a user interacts with the kiosk.

## 3. Why Does PrintBit Process Your Information?

PrintBit processes information only for specific and legitimate purposes connected to the operation of the service, including:

- Processing and completing printing, copying, and scanning requests
- Identifying and associating a transaction with its session or customer identifier
- Calculating applicable service charges
- Recording transaction history and operational records
- Providing document retrieval or QR-based document transfer features, where applicable
- Detecting and resolving failed or interrupted transactions
- Monitoring printer, scanner, and kiosk hardware status
- Maintaining system security and preventing unauthorized use
- Investigating technical problems, disputes, and service incidents
- Meeting applicable legal, accounting, or regulatory requirements

PrintBit will not use personal information for an unrelated purpose that is incompatible with the purpose for which it was collected unless a lawful basis exists and applicable requirements are satisfied.

## 4. How Is Your Information Processed?

PrintBit is primarily designed to process kiosk data locally on the device and its connected systems where technically feasible.

Personal information may be processed through:

- The PrintBit kiosk application
- The local printing and scanning components
- Local transaction and operational databases
- Administrative functions used by authorized personnel
- QR-based document retrieval or transfer mechanisms, where applicable
- Authorized service providers or infrastructure used to operate a particular PrintBit deployment, where applicable

The exact processing environment may differ depending on the deployment of the PrintBit kiosk.

## 5. Uploaded, Printed, Copied, and Scanned Documents

PrintBit may temporarily process the contents of documents submitted to the kiosk.

These files may contain personal, confidential, or sensitive information that was not directly requested by PrintBit. PrintBit processes such information only as necessary to provide the service requested by the user.

Temporary document files should be deleted or securely disposed of after the applicable transaction or retention period has ended.

Users should avoid submitting documents containing unnecessary personal information.

## 6. QR Codes and Document Retrieval

Some PrintBit features may use QR codes to allow a user to retrieve or transfer a document.

Where this feature is enabled:

- The QR code may function as a means of accessing the associated document or transaction.
- Users should not intentionally share a document-retrieval QR code with unauthorized individuals.
- Access to a document through a QR code may remain available only for the configured retrieval period.
- Retrieved or temporary documents should be deleted when the applicable retention period ends.

PrintBit should not place unnecessary personal information directly inside a QR code. Where technically possible, QR codes should contain only a temporary identifier or retrieval reference.

## 7. Legal Basis for Processing

PrintBit processes personal information only when there is a lawful basis under applicable data protection law.

Depending on the specific processing activity, the lawful basis may include:

- Consent of the data subject, where consent is required
- Processing necessary to provide a service requested by the user
- Compliance with a legal obligation
- Protection of legitimate interests, where permitted by law
- Other lawful bases recognized under applicable laws and regulations

Where consent is relied upon, users will be provided with appropriate information before the relevant processing takes place.

Providing a Privacy Notice does not, by itself, constitute consent to processing.
