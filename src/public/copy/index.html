      Prepare your document

      To avoid paper jams and keep your preview clear:

      • Use clean, flat, undamaged bond paper.
      • Remove staples, clips, tape, folded areas, and torn edges.
      • For the ADF, align pages straight. On the glass, place the document flat.

      ADF Paper Jam Removal Directions

      Step 1

      1. Open the ADF cover

      Caution: Make sure you open the ADF cover before removing any jammed paper. Otherwise the product may be damaged.

      Step 2

      2. Carefully remove any jammed paper

      Gently pull the jammed sheet out from the feeder. Ensure no torn scraps remain inside.

      Step 3

      3. Open the ADF input tray

      Caution: Make sure you open the ADF cover before opening the input tray. Otherwise the product may be damaged.

      Step 4

      4. Carefully remove jammed paper from beneath tray

      Gently remove any jammed paper from beneath the input tray.

      Step 5

      5. Close tray (1) and ADF cover (2)

      Close the ADF input tray first (1), then close the ADF cover firmly until it clicks (2). Reload flat paper and tap Retry.

      Clear the Printer Error First

      Step 1

      1. Check the printer's LED / screen

      Look at the printer's control panel. A red or blinking LED, or an error icon on the printer screen, means the printer is waiting for you to act on it.

      Step 2

      2. Press the printer's paper / stop / OK button

      Press the paper/stop button (or the OK/start button) on the printer itself to acknowledge and clear the error. The kiosk cannot dismiss it for you.

      Step 3

      3. Clear any jam shown on the printer

      Caution: Always open the ADF cover before removing jammed paper. Otherwise the printer may be damaged.

      Step 4

      4. Wait until the printer LED is steady (green / ready)

      The printer is only ready when its LED is steady — no red light, no blinking. Wait a few seconds for it to finish resetting.

      Step 5

      5. Return to this kiosk and tap Retry

      Once the printer is ready, come back to this screen and tap Retry. Reload the Long Bond page face-up in the ADF if you removed it.
