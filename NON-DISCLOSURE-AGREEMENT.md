## 2. Background and Purpose

**WHEREAS**, Disclosing Party has conceived, engineered, and built **PrintBit**, a proprietary coin-operated, self-service printing kiosk system integrating Node.js/Express/TypeScript software, C# .NET hardware worker services, LibreOffice automated document conversion pipelines, ESP32 captive-portal microcontroller firmware, pulse-driven coin acceptors, coin hopper change dispensers, and peripheral drivers; and

**WHEREAS**, the Parties wish to engage in confidential discussions regarding a potential commercial partnership, joint venture, investment, component fabrication, hardware assembly, distribution, or technical collaboration (the **"Authorized Purpose"**); and

**WHEREAS**, in connection with such discussions, Disclosing Party may reveal certain non-public, technical, commercial, financial, and proprietary information to Receiving Party.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

## 3. Definition of Confidential Information

**"Confidential Information"** shall include all non-public, sensitive, or proprietary information disclosed by Disclosing Party to Receiving Party, whether disclosed orally, visually, in writing, electronically, or through inspection of tangible objects, prototypes, or systems, including but not limited to:

1. **Source Code and Software Architecture**: All source code, scripts, binaries, algorithms, data models, state machines, pipe protocols, database schemas, and software documentation across the Node.js backend, C# Worker service, and frontend kiosk UI.
2. **Firmware and Microcontroller Code**: All firmware code, SoftAP captive portal logic, GPIO pin assignments, hardware interrupt timing routines, pulse-width detection algorithms, and communication protocols between the ESP32 and host industrial PC.
3. **Hardware Schematics and Mechanical Designs**: Electrical schematics, power supply wiring, relay wiring diagrams, multi-coin acceptor pulse configurations, coin hopper wiring, chassis blueprints, fabrication specifications, bill of materials (BOM), and component sourcing lists.
4. **Product Roadmaps and Specifications**: Technical whitepapers, unreleased feature architectures, bug reports, performance metrics, testing suites, security audits, and deployment guides.
5. **Business, Financial, and Operational Data**: Kiosk build costs, revenue-sharing models, coin intake telemetry, operational margin calculations, customer pricing algorithms, vendor agreements, and expansion strategies.
6. **Customer and Venue Information**: Institutional host relationships, site locations, customer transaction logs, and operational audit reports.

Confidential Information need not be explicitly labeled as "Confidential" or "Proprietary" to be protected hereunder, provided that a reasonable person under the circumstances would recognize the information as confidential.

---

## 4. Exclusions from Confidential Information

Confidential Information shall not include any information that Receiving Party can demonstrate through verifiable written records:

1. Was already known to Receiving Party without confidentiality restrictions prior to disclosure by Disclosing Party;
2. Is or becomes publicly known and generally available through no breach of this Agreement or wrongful act by Receiving Party;
3. Was received by Receiving Party from an independent third party who had the legal right to disclose such information without restriction; or
4. Was independently developed by Receiving Party without reference to, reliance upon, or use of Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information.

---

## 5. Non-Disclosure and Use Obligations

Receiving Party agrees to:

1. **Strict Confidentiality**: Hold all Confidential Information in the strictest confidence and protect it using at least the same degree of care it uses for its own most sensitive information, but no less than a high and reasonable standard of care.
2. **Limited Purpose**: Use the Confidential Information solely for the Authorized Purpose and for no other commercial, technical, competitive, or personal purpose.
3. **No Reverse Engineering**: Explicitly refrain from reverse engineering, decompiling, disassembling, modifying, cloning, analyzing electrical pulses of, or attempting to derive the source code, firmware logic, or physical schematics of the PrintBit system or any component thereof.
4. **Prohibition on Competing Works**: Not use any Confidential Information to develop, design, fabricate, or market any competing coin-operated printing kiosk or derivative system.
5. **Restricted Dissemination**: Restrict disclosure of Confidential Information strictly to its directors, officers, employees, and professional legal/financial advisers who:
   - Need to know the information to carry out the Authorized Purpose;
   - Have been informed of the confidential nature of the information; and
   - Are bound by written confidentiality agreements no less restrictive than this Agreement.
6. **Notification of Breach**: Immediately notify Disclosing Party in writing upon discovering any unauthorized disclosure, loss, or theft of Confidential Information, and cooperate fully in recovering and mitigating such breach.

---

## 6. Legally Compelled Disclosures

If Receiving Party is compelled by valid court order, subpoena, or government authority pursuant to Philippine law to disclose any Confidential Information:

1. Receiving Party shall provide prompt written notice to Disclosing Party prior to disclosure, allowing Disclosing Party reasonable opportunity to seek a protective order or confidential treatment.
2. Receiving Party shall disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that is legally mandated to be disclosed.
3. Such legally compelled disclosure shall not relieve Receiving Party of its confidentiality obligations with respect to all other uses or disclosures.

---

## 7. Return or Destruction of Materials

Upon written request by Disclosing Party or upon termination of the discussions regarding the Authorized Purpose:

1. Receiving Party shall promptly (and in no event later than five (5) business days) return to Disclosing Party or permanently destroy (at Disclosing Party's option) all copies, summaries, extracts, diagrams, code repositories, schematics, prototypes, and notes containing or derived from Confidential Information.
2. An authorized officer of Receiving Party shall certify in writing that all such materials have been returned or securely and permanently destroyed.

## 8. Intellectual Property & No License Granted

1. **Retention of Rights**: All Confidential Information remains the exclusive intellectual and proprietary property of Disclosing Party and its contributors under Republic Act No. 8293 (Intellectual Property Code of the Philippines).
2. **No License Conveyed**: Nothing contained in this Agreement shall be construed as granting, either expressly, implicitly, by estoppel or otherwise, any license, right, title, or interest in or to any patent, copyright, trademark, trade secret, or other intellectual property of Disclosing Party.
3. **No Obligation**: This Agreement does not obligate either Party to proceed with any commercial transaction, contract, joint venture, or business relationship.

---

## 9. Term and Survival

1. **Term**: This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of three (3) years from the Effective Date, unless terminated earlier by mutual written agreement.
2. **Survival of Confidentiality**: Receiving Party's obligations of confidentiality, non-disclosure, and non-use with respect to:
   - General commercial, operational, and financial information shall survive for a period of five (5) years following the termination or expiration of this Agreement.
   - Source code, firmware, hardware schematics, trade secrets, and core software architecture shall survive **in perpetuity** or for as long as such information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

---

## 10. Remedies and Injunctive Relief

Receiving Party acknowledges and agrees that:

1. Any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement will cause irreparable injury, competitive harm, and significant financial damage to Disclosing Party for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate.
2. In the event of any breach or threatened breach, Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek immediate injunctive relief, temporary restraining orders, and specific performance in any court of competent jurisdiction, without the requirement of posting a bond or proving actual monetary damages.
3. Such equitable remedies shall be in addition to, and not in lieu of, any other legal or monetary damages, including attorney's fees and litigation costs, to which Disclosing Party may be entitled under the law.
